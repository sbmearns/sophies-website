02 / Creative Practice

Digital Media & Visual Culture

An interest in the visual language of media and the ways photography, design, and editorial storytelling shape cultural narratives and collective memory.

Editorial & Graphic Design

Visual communication, typography, graphic design, and editorial composition. Interested in the relationship between design, publishing, and cultural documentation.

03 / Academic Interests

Research & Writing

Research at the intersection of archival ethics, gender, representation, and the politics of historical memory. Interested in who gets documented, who controls the record, and whose histories become accessible.

Gender & the Historical Record

Women's history, feminist archives, queer communities, and the ways marginalized groups document their own experiences in response to historical exclusion.

Archival Ethics & Privacy

Privacy, consent, digitization, and institutional responsibility when providing access to sensitive historical materials and personal information.

Reproductive Justice & Borders

Gender, immigration enforcement, reproductive autonomy, and the role of institutional power in shaping access to healthcare and bodily autonomy.

Periodicals as Cultural Memory

Magazines and independent publications as historical records of women's identities, sexuality, fashion, politics, and cultural movements.
