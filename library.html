The Santa Cruz Public Library

At the Santa Cruz Public Library, I developed archival workflows and tracked team efficiency and progress through meetings and detailed logs. I was carefully handling fragile materials,creating metadata, and uploading digitized assets to public digital collections for open access.Some memorable materials I digitized were century old scrapbooks belonging to female naturalist, Laura Hecox and magazines documenting the 1970s Bay Area feminist and LGBTQ movements.

Digitized Publication: Definitely Biased

As part of my digitization work, I worked with Definitely Biased, a feminist publication documenting women's writing, art, poetry, and community perspectives. Digitizing this periodical involved preserving a piece of feminist print culture and making its contents more accessible for research and discovery.
